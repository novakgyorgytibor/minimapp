A MinimApp egy minimalista navigáció: fekete-fehér térkép, amin szinte csak az útvonalad látszik. Nincs zsúfolt képernyő, nincs reklám, nincs fiók.

## English

**Name** (30): `MinimApp`

**Subtitle – App Store** (30): `Minimalist navigation`

**Short description – Play** (80): `Black-and-white navigation that shows little more than your route. No ads.`

**Description** (4000):

```
MinimApp is minimalist navigation: a black-and-white map that shows little more than your route. No cluttered screen, no ads, no account.

• Driving, cycling and walking
• Choose between two routes: next to the fastest one, tap the faint alternative to switch
• Toll-free alternative when every route would use a toll road
• Turn-by-turn instructions, lane guidance and speed limit sign
• Reroutes automatically when you leave the route
• Favourite places: save them with the heart and find them right away in search
• The part you've travelled turns grey, the road ahead stays white
• Hungarian and English

Privacy: I don't collect or store anything about you. No analytics, no tracking. Your favourites and language setting stay on your phone.

Maps and routing: open OpenStreetMap-based services (OpenFreeMap, Valhalla, Photon). Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
```
